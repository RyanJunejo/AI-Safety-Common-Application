# Invite for survey respondents (draft)

Send this once, from a person's own address, to people who answered the survey. It invites them to try the site; it doesn't sign anyone up. Alerts stay off until each person turns them on.

---

**Subject:** Thanks for the survey: SafetyApps is open to try

Hi {first name},

Thanks again for answering our survey about applying to AI safety programs. Many of you said the hard part was knowing what's open and when, so we built that first.

**[SafetyApps](https://safetyapps.org)** lists AI safety fellowships and other programs, courses, jobs, and communities in one place, updated daily from AISafety.com. You can:

- Search and filter by focus (technical, governance, general), country, or remote.
- Save anything to your list, no account needed.
- Sign in with just your email to keep the list on every device. If you want, turn on alerts: a Monday digest of new matches, reminders 7 and 2 days before deadlines you've saved, and an email when a program you follow opens a new round.

It's a public beta, so we'd love to hear what's missing or wrong. Just reply to this email.

We haven't added you to any mailing list. This is the only email you'll get from us unless you turn on alerts yourself.

Thanks,
{name}
SafetyApps

---

## Before sending

- Send from a personal mailbox or a one-off send, not through the alerts system, and send only to people who gave their email in the survey.
- Remove anyone who asked not to be contacted.
- Check the links on the live domain first.
